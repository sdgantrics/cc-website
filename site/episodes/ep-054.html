Lead With the Answer: How Site Leaders Lose the Room in 90 Seconds

Libby Magliolo · Founder and Presentation Skills Trainer · The Slide Master

In this episode, Libby Magliolo, founder of The Slide Master, reveals how mastering the art of presentation and strategic communication can propel you from good to great, especially in industries like construction where technical expertise isn’t enough. If you've ever felt lost trying to convey a complex idea or worried your message isn’t landing, this episode is your game-changer.

General Superintendent - Lessons in Leadership and How to Future Proof Your Career with Mitch McDonald

In this episode, we are joined by Mitch McDonald, General Superintendent at XL Construction, as he shares his fascinating journey from a third-generation construction family to leading major projects. Mitch dives into his early life, growing up on a dairy farm, and how those experiences shaped his work ethic and career in construction. We explore Mitch's rapid rise in the industry, his transition from field to office roles, and the invaluable lessons learned along the way. He offers insightful advice on balancing work-life rhythms, the importance of mentorship, and how young professionals can navigate their careers in construction. Mitch also discusses the future of the construction industry, emphasizing the critical need for skilled labor and the potential career paths for those entering the field.

Sr. Superintendent - Is Tech Failing to Improve the Lives of Project Teams with Wes Juergen

Welcome to the first episode of Construction Conversations with our host, Stephen Poppe. In this insightful discussion, we're joined by Wes Juergens, a Senior Superintendent at Austin Commercial. Wes imparts valuable knowledge onto listeners, discussing critical topics like the ongoing labor shortage, the integration of much-needed technology in the industry, and how evolving tech is reshaping project completion. The dialogue progresses to focus on technology's profound effects on the construction sector. Wes elaborates on how advanced tech, such as AI and robotics, can potentially revolutionize the industry and provides practical, tangible examples. He also explains the immense benefits of software programs in managing projects and solving issues. A compelling part of the episode is when Wes speaks about the impacts of technology on work-life balance.

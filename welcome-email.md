Hey there,

Look at you, joining a construction newsletter. Bold move.

Every week, I'll drop a Construction Brief in your inbox, short, smart, and hopefully a fun read on what's working, what's broken, and what's coming next in this wild industry we love.

You'll get stories from the field, ideas worth stealing, and a few unfiltered takes from me and other industry leaders I am blessed to call friends. Sometimes I'll throw in a tool, tech rec, or podcast episode that's too good to gatekeep.

One thing you get right now, though. Over 50-some episodes of the podcast, the people on it have handed over the lessons that cost them projects, clients, and in a couple of cases careers. We put the 50 sharpest into one deck, each card with what it cost, their exact words, and the move to make next time. It's built for toolbox talks, print one and read it at a morning huddle.
